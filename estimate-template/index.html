US rules that apply to each page

Words such as estimate, quote and bid have no single legal meaning across the US. What binds you depends on the wording and the state. How much over an estimate can a contractor go shows two state examples.

How to choose

Start from the trade closest to the job, because its sample lines, exclusions and rules are already filled in. When the customer says yes, the invoice pages take the same lines. Estimate vs invoice explains when to send each paper.
