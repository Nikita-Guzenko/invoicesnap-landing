What a cleaning estimate should say

A cleaning estimate sets the rhythm of the service. It tells the client the rooms, the task list, how often you come and who supplies what.

FieldWhy it matters before the first visit
Frequency and hoursWeekly, biweekly or nightly. After-hours access is a cost.
Task list per roomIt prevents "I thought that was included".
Supplies and equipmentSay who buys them, and who buys the paper goods.
First deep cleanThe first visit often takes longer, so price it apart.
Access and keysWho opens the door, and the alarm code process.
Notice periodHow either side ends a recurring plan.

Estimate, quote or bid: US law gives these words no single meaning. Your wording and your state decide what binds you. Write "fixed price", or say how the price can change. See estimate vs invoice.

Rules to check before you send a cleaning estimate

Add tax in Texas

A Texas client pays tax on building cleaning, in a home or an office, with an exception for a self-employed housekeeper employed by the household. Show the tax before signing. Texas Comptroller 94-111

Taxable for a business client too

Texas names office, warehouse, garage and restaurant cleaning in the same rule, so a commercial estimate there carries the tax line as well. Texas Comptroller 94-111

Training claims

BLS says that janitors and building cleaners do not need certification. Say "certified" on an estimate only when you can name the certificate. BLS

Cleaning estimate questions

How do I price a house that I have not seen?

Give a range by bedrooms and bathrooms, and confirm after a walk-through. Mark it clearly as an estimate.

How do I quote a commercial job?

Price per visit, then multiply by visits per month. Set the deposit to empty when you bill monthly in advance.

Should I show a price per square foot?

Use it for offices if your client compares bids that way. Homes usually compare by rooms.

What if the client wants supplies included?

Add a supplies line, or write that you bring standard products. Say who pays for special products.
