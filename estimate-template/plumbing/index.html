What a plumbing estimate should say

The estimate tells the customer what you will do, what you will not do, the price, and how long the price holds. Hidden pipe work is the usual source of a price dispute, so say how you treat it.

FieldWhy it matters before the wall is open
Scope, room by roomThe customer can compare your price with another bid on the same work.
ExclusionsList what the price does not cover, such as tile, drywall finish and fixtures.
Hidden conditionsSay what happens when you find corroded pipe or a bad vent behind the wall.
Fixture allowanceName the model, or give an allowance and say who pays the difference.
Permit and inspectionSay who pulls the permit and whether the fee is in the price.
Valid-until dateSet a date so that the price does not stay open.
Deposit and payment stepsLink payments to steps such as rough-in passed and final connection.

Estimate, quote or bid: Say what your paper means. Write "fixed price", or say how the price can change. Ask your state board or a lawyer what binds you. See estimate vs invoice.

Rules to check before you send a plumbing estimate

Who may do the work

In Texas, anyone except a responsible master plumber needs the proper license, registration or endorsement, and licensed supervision. Put your number on the estimate when your state asks. Texas Occupations Code 1301.351

Permit rules are local

Permit rules are local. Kirkland, Washington, asks for a mechanical permit on a gas water heater swap and a plumbing permit when you move water piping, so check each city. City of Kirkland

Sales at the customer home

A buyer may cancel a home sale until midnight of the third business day under the FTC Cooling-Off Rule. The rule has exceptions, such as a repair visit that the buyer asked for. Ask a lawyer about your case. 16 CFR 429.1

Plumbing estimate questions

Is a plumbing estimate the same as a quote?

Write "estimate" and state how the price can change, or write "fixed price" if it will not change.

Should I itemize parts on an estimate?

Itemize when the customer compares bids. Show one lot price when your supplier cost changes often. Keep labor apart either way, so that you can use the tax choice on the form.

How long should a plumbing estimate stay valid?

Choose a date that matches how fast your supplier prices change. The form prints the date from the number of days you enter.

Can I charge for writing an estimate?

Say so before the visit. A paid diagnostic visit is a separate charge from a free written price. Credit it to the job if you promised that.
