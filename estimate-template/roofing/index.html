What a roofing estimate should say

A roofing estimate is mostly measurements. Give the roof size in squares, the layers to remove, the materials by name, and how you will price the decking that you cannot see yet.

FieldWhy it matters on a roof
Roof size in squaresOne square is 100 sq ft. The customer can compare bids on the same size.
Layers to tear offEach added layer costs more to remove and dispose of.
Shingle maker and gradeA plain word such as "shingles" invites disputes.
Decking price per sheetThe damage is unknown until tear-off, so agree on a unit price now.
Workmanship warrantyKeep it apart from the maker warranty on the material.
Insurance wordingSay that the owner pays the deductible. Florida prohibits offers to waive one.

Estimate, quote or bid: US law gives these words no single meaning. Your wording and your state decide what binds you. Write "fixed price", or say how the price can change. See estimate vs invoice.

Rules to check before you send a roofing estimate

Florida: no waived deductible

Florida Statutes 489.147 lists as prohibited an offer to a residential owner of a rebate, gift, cash or waiver of an insurance deductible for allowing a roof inspection or making a claim. Florida Statutes 489.147

California: down payment

The cap on a home improvement job is the lesser of $1,000 or 10 percent, except for blanket-bond licensees, so a large roof deposit may be far below 10 percent. CSLB guide

Door-to-door roof sales

After a sale at the home, a buyer may cancel until midnight of the third business day, under the FTC Cooling-Off Rule. Add the notice when you sign after a door-knock. 16 CFR 429.1

Roofing estimate questions

Should I show insurance estimate amounts on my estimate?

Show your own price for your own scope. If the customer shares the adjuster paperwork, answer it in a separate document and do not offer to absorb the deductible.

How do I price decking that I cannot see?

Give a unit price per sheet or per square foot and an allowance. Bill what you replace after tear-off, and take a photo of each sheet.

Do I measure in squares or square feet?

Use what your crew and supplier use, and say which unit. The form has a unit field for each line.

How long should a roofing estimate be valid?

Many shops use a short period because shingle prices move. The sample uses 14 days, and you can change it in the form.
