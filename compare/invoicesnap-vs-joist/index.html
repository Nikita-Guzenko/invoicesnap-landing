InvoiceSnap vs Joist

Joist is software for contractors, with tools to estimate jobs, send invoices and get paid.

The InvoiceSnap browser form makes one invoice or estimate file that you download and send yourself.

What Joist lists on its features page

Ask Joist which plan has each feature.

  • Joist builds detailed estimates with clear pricing.
  • Joist turns approved estimates into invoices.
  • Joist lets customers pay with multiple payment options.
  • Joist schedules visits and organizes jobs.
  • Joist keeps customer details, estimates, invoices and job history.
  • Joist captures receipts and expenses.
  • Joist works together with QuickBooks.

What the InvoiceSnap browser form does

  • You fill in an invoice or an estimate in the browser, with no account.
  • You download the document as a PDF, Word or Excel file and send it yourself.
  • The form does not take payments, schedule visits or track expenses.
  • The form does not keep your customers for your next visit. Download the file before you close the page.

Which one fits your job

Look at Joist if you also want visit scheduling and job history, not only documents.

Use the InvoiceSnap form if you need one estimate or invoice now and you already have a way to get paid. Enter your own prices and check the file before you send it.

What this page does not compare

This page does not compare prices, speed or support, and it gives no score. InvoiceSnap is our product, so read the Joist page yourself before you choose.

Short answers

Does the InvoiceSnap form take card payments?

No. It makes a document. Put your payment details on it, and record the payment when it arrives.

Can I move from the form to an app later?

Yes. Keep the files that you downloaded. Enter your customers again in the app that you choose.
