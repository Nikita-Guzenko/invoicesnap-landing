InvoiceSnap vs Jobber

Jobber calls itself the all-in-one solution for home service pros.

The InvoiceSnap browser form is a document tool: it makes an invoice or estimate file and nothing more.

Features that Jobber names

Ask Jobber which plan has each feature.

  • Jobber schedules work and tells your customers and crew about it.
  • Jobber can assign new work to the closest team member by GPS location.
  • Jobber quotes are interactive: customers can add services and approve the new total.
  • Jobber generates invoices from the job details, with payment options.
  • Jobber offers customers online payment.
  • Jobber tracks your customer info in one place.

Where the InvoiceSnap form stops

The form has no crew, no calendar and no map. It gives you a PDF, Word or Excel file of one invoice or estimate. You send that file by e-mail or text, and you collect the money your own way.

There is no sign-in, and the form keeps no customer list. If you close the tab, the entries are gone.

How to decide

  1. Count the people who do the jobs. If your team needs dispatch, look at a system like Jobber.
  2. Count the invoices each week. If you send a few invoices, a document form can be enough.
  3. Try one real job in each tool and look at the result on a phone.

Limits of this page

This page does not compare prices, speed or support, and it gives no score. InvoiceSnap is our product, so read the Jobber page yourself before you choose.

Short answers

Is the InvoiceSnap form a Jobber replacement?

No. It makes documents only. It does not schedule, dispatch or take payments.

Can I make a quote with InvoiceSnap?

Yes. Use the estimate form and download the file.
