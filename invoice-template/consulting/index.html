Tax and contract rules for consultants

How clients report you

The IRS instructions tell a business to report nonemployee compensation of $2,000 or more on Form 1099-NEC. Your invoices are the record that you check the form against. IRS 1099-NEC instructions

Form W-9

The IRS says that you give Form W-9 so that the person who files the information return has your correct taxpayer number. IRS Form W-9

Estimated tax

Sole proprietors and others who expect to owe $1,000 or more at filing generally pay estimated tax. A retainer does not hold tax back for you, so set money aside per invoice. IRS estimated taxes

Consulting invoice questions

Should I bill a retainer in advance?

Do what your agreement says. Write the period on the line, for example November advisory, so that the client can book it to the right month.

How much detail goes on an hourly bill?

Enough for the client to approve it. Show date ranges and topic, or attach a time log and name it in the notes.

Do I add expenses with a markup?

Follow your agreement. If you bill travel at cost, add receipts and say so in the notes.

Do I need a separate invoice for each statement of work?

It helps the client route the payment. Use one invoice per SOW unless the client asks for one bill per month.
