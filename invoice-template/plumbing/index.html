What a plumbing invoice must show

A plumbing invoice shows who did the work, where and when, each part and each labor charge, the tax, the amount due and how to pay. The US has no single federal list of invoice fields. The IRS names invoices as records that support your gross receipts (IRS), so keep each one.

FieldWhy it matters on a plumbing job
Your business name, address, phoneThe customer and your bank can identify you.
Contractor license numberMost states and some localities license plumbers (BLS). Ask your state board what must print on paper.
Service addressThe job address is often not the billing address, for example with a landlord.
Invoice number, invoice date, service dateEach number is unique. The service date starts any warranty on the work.
Labor lines and parts lines, apartSales tax can apply to parts and labor differently, by state.
Trip fee, after-hours charge, permit feeA separate line prevents a dispute at the door. Say if the trip fee is credited to the repair.
Deposit paid, balance due, due date, how to payThe customer sees one clear amount and one clear date.
Warranty on parts and laborState the period, or state that there is none.

Plumbing rules that touch the invoice

License

Most states and some localities license plumbers, so the number on your paper depends on the state. In Texas, a person other than a responsible master plumber may not do plumbing without the required license, registration or endorsement, and needs supervision by a licensed person. Texas Occupations Code 1301.351

Permit on a water heater

Permit rules are local. In Kirkland, Washington, replacing a gas water heater needs a mechanical permit, and moving water piping needs a plumbing permit. When you pull a permit, put the fee on its own line. City of Kirkland

Sales tax on repair labor

One state can treat homes and businesses differently. In Texas, labor to repair residential real property is not taxable, but the total charge for a nonresidential repair is. The form lets you tax parts only or the full amount. Texas Comptroller 94-116

Plumbing invoice questions

Can I charge a trip fee and hourly labor on one invoice?

Yes, if you told the customer before the visit. Write the trip fee on its own line. If you credit it against the repair, write that in the notes.

Do I show my cost for parts or my price?

Show your price to the customer. Your markup does not need its own line. Keep the supplier receipts for your records.

Should the invoice list the permit number?

Write the permit number in the notes when the job had a permit. It gives the customer a record for a later sale of the home.

What do I do when a customer disputes the after-hours rate?

Point to the line on your signed estimate or the rate you gave on the phone. Keep the after-hours charge on its own line, so that the customer can see it apart from the labor.
