What to show on a handyman invoice

A handyman invoice lists small tasks clearly, shows how you bill time and material, and states your license status where a state asks.

FieldWhy it matters on small jobs
One line per taskGive each small job its own line.
Minimum chargeShow it on its own line when the job was shorter than your minimum.
Materials and markupSay if you add a markup, so the receipt does not surprise the customer.
License statusIn California, the job size can decide whether you need a contractor license.

Payment methodName Zelle, check or card. Say if you expect payment on the day.

Handyman rules that touch the invoice

California: the $1,000 line

Since 1 January 2025, California exempts minor work up to $1,000 from a contractor license. The work cannot need a permit, and the unlicensed person cannot employ anyone on the project. CSLB Bulletin 24-07

California: say so in ads

An unlicensed person there may advertise for work up to $1,000 only if the ad says the person is not licensed. You can print the same line on the invoice. CSLB Bulletin 24-07

Florida: casual, minor work

Florida exempts casual, minor or inconsequential work under $2,500 total from 489.103's contractor licensing part. Larger jobs, split contracts and anyone who advertises as a contractor do not qualify. Florida Statutes 489.103(9)

Handyman invoice questions

Should I charge by the hour or by the job?

Use the hour for mixed lists and the job for tasks that you know well. The form takes both: set the unit field to hour or job.

How do I show a minimum charge?

Add a line named Minimum service charge. Then the customer sees why a 20-minute visit costs more than 20 minutes of your rate.

Do I need sales tax on a handyman invoice?

Leave the rate empty when you do not charge it. Ask your state revenue office if you are not sure.

What goes in the license field when I do not have one?

Do not write a number that you do not hold. Write your exemption, or leave the field empty if your state does not ask for one.
