What to show on an HVAC invoice

An HVAC invoice ties the charge to one piece of equipment. The parts below stop most warranty and refrigerant questions later.

FieldWhy it matters on HVAC work
Equipment make, model and serial numberWarranty claims and the next technician need them. Write them in the notes.
Refrigerant type and amountShow what the pound charge is for.
Technician certificationEPA Section 608 applies to work that could release refrigerant.
Diagnostic fee and repair, apartSay if the fee is credited to the repair.

HVAC rules that touch the invoice

Refrigerant certification

EPA says technicians who maintain, service, repair or dispose of equipment that could release refrigerant must pass a test for Section 608 certification. You can print it on the invoice. US EPA Section 608

Equipment made before 2025

A rule effective 27 July 2026 ends the install deadline for residential and light commercial AC and heat pump systems if all specified parts were made or imported before 2025. Write the manufacture date in the notes when you install older stock. Federal Register 2026-10387

Sales tax in one example state

In Texas, labor to repair residential real property is not taxable. The publication is from 2007 and does not name HVAC, so confirm with the state before you apply it. Texas Comptroller 94-116

HVAC invoice questions

Do I charge the diagnostic fee when the customer declines the repair?

Tell the customer the fee before the visit, then bill it on its own line. Bill it alone and note that the customer declined the repair.

How do I bill refrigerant?

Bill by the pound on its own line, with the type. The form has a unit field, so type lb and the amount you added.

Should a maintenance visit have a different invoice?

The fields are the same. Name the plan and the visit number in the notes, so the customer can track what the plan covers.

Where do I put the model and serial number?

Use the notes field. They print under the totals on the PDF and in the Word file.
