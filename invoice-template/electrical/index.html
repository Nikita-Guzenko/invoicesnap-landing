What to show on an electrical invoice

An electrical invoice shows who did the work under which license, what changed in the panel or circuit, and which permit covers it.

FieldWhy it matters on electrical work
License number of the contractorMost states require electricians to be licensed.
Permit number and inspection resultThe number lets the owner find the record.
Labor by license levelApprentice, journeyman and master rates differ, so each gets a line.
Circuit and panel detailAmps, breaker and room make the later service call shorter.
Materials, apart from laborThe form can tax parts only, or all.
Warranty periodSay how long your labor warranty runs.

Electrical rules that touch the invoice

Texas license

Texas generally requires a Chapter 1305 license for electrical work. Licensed plumbers may disconnect or reconnect plumbing fixtures and appliances on existing circuits, but may do no other electrical work. Texas TDLR

Washington permits

Electrical work there needs a permit and an inspection, with a few exceptions. Put the permit number in the notes field. Washington L&I

Local inspection

Texas TDLR does not inspect for local permits. The local authority does. Name the city or county on your invoice. Texas TDLR

Electrical invoice questions

Should I bill a flat rate or hourly?

Both work on this form. Enter one job line with quantity 1 for a flat rate, or enter hours and an hourly rate per license level.

Do I list the breaker and wire on the invoice?

List them if the customer asks. For a small repair, you can use one materials line.

What if a permit fee changes after the estimate?

Bill the real fee on its own line and keep the receipt from the city.

Can I show an apprentice and a journeyman on one job?

Give each person a line with hours and rate.
