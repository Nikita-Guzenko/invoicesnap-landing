What to show on a freelance writing invoice

The invoice shows the work by word count or hours, the rights that pass, the due date and how to pay. Add a PO number if the client asks for one.

Rights and tax rules for writers

Copyright transfer

Under 17 U.S.C. 204, a transfer of copyright is not valid unless it is in writing and signed by the owner or the owner's agent. An invoice that you sign can be evidence, but your contract should hold the transfer. 17 U.S.C. 204

Work made for hire

17 U.S.C. 101 limits this to employees and to commissioned works in listed categories with a signed written agreement. Ask a lawyer before you write work for hire on an invoice. 17 U.S.C. 101

Form 1099-NEC

The IRS instructions call for reporting nonemployee compensation of $2,000 or more. Check the instructions for the tax year. Keep your own invoices for every client below that amount, too. IRS 1099-NEC instructions

Freelance writer invoice questions

Do I bill the word count I wrote or the published count?

Use the count in your contract. If it says nothing, use the delivered count and state it on the line.

How do I write a per-word rate on the form?

Enter the word count as the quantity, word as the unit and the rate in dollars, such as 0.25. The total calculates by itself.

When do I add a kill fee?

When the client stops a piece after you started and your contract has a kill fee. Add it as its own line with the article title.

Can I bill for research time?

Check your agreement. Show research hours on a separate line, or include them in your per-word or project price.
