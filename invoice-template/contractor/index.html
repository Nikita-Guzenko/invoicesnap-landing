What to show on a contractor invoice

A construction invoice links the amount to the contract: which stage, which change order, what the customer already paid, and what remains.

Contractor license numberLets the owner check your license. California asks for it on the contract.

Change ordersEach one gets its own line with its number and date.

Contractor rules that touch the invoice

Down payment cap in California

On home improvement jobs, the down payment cannot pass the lesser of $1,000 or 10 percent, except for blanket-bond licensees. Later payments cannot exceed the value of work performed. CSLB guide

Change orders in writing

The California CSLB guide says that a price change must be a written change order in the contract. New Jersey requires written changes to home improvement contracts over $500. N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2

Lien rights

In California, suppliers and subcontractors give the owner a Preliminary Notice within 20 days of delivering material or starting work. Contractors give a Mechanics Lien Warning. CSLB guide

Contractor invoice questions

How do I bill a progress payment?

Enter one line per stage with quantity 1. Put the stage in the description, enter past payments in the form, and the file prints the balance due.

Is this form for a 1099 independent contractor?

Yes. A self-employed person who bills a business can use it. Use the freelancer page if you bill by hours or by project with no parts.

Should each change order have its own invoice?

Choose what your contract allows. You can add a line to the next progress bill with the change order number, so one document shows the running total.

Can I bill for material that is on site but not installed?

Your contract decides this. In California, payments cannot exceed the value of the performed work, so check this before you bill material that is not installed.
