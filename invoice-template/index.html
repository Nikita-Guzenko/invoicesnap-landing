Invoice templates

Pick your trade. Each page has a form, a live paper and PDF, Word and Excel downloads, plus line items and rules for that work.

US rules that apply to each page

The IRS says that, except in a few cases, the law does not require any special kind of records. Each trade page links the state examples that it uses. What to include on an invoice has the general field list.

How to choose

Pick the page for the work that you bill most often, because its sample lines and rules are closest to your job. If you do two trades, use one page and change the lines: every form takes any text.
