What to show on a freelance invoice

A freelance invoice says who you are, what you delivered, what is left to pay, by when, and how. It also says what the client owns. The IRS names invoices among the supporting documents that you keep for your books.

FieldWhy it matters when you work alone
Your legal name or business nameUse the name on your W-9, so that a finance team can match the payment.
What you deliveredName files, pages or hours, not only "services".
Deposit and balanceShow what the client already paid.
Due dateNew York law sets a default of 30 days when the contract sets no date.
RightsState what transfers, and when.
Late feePrint only what your contract allows.

Freelance rules that touch the invoice

New York State

With no payment date in the contract, payment is due within 30 days after you finish (section 1411). Section 1410 sets an $800 floor, over 120 days, and excludes construction contractors. NY GBL 1410

New York City

The city says that contracts worth $800 or more must be in writing, with the work, the pay and the payment date. It says that a worker may collect double the unpaid amount for late payment. NYC DCWP

California

SB 988 requires a written contract for professional services of $250 or more, with payment due within 30 days after completion if the contract sets no date. California SB 988 bill page

Freelance invoice questions

What should I do when a client asks for a W-9?

Send it. Form W-9 gives the person who files an information return your correct taxpayer number.

How do I show a deposit that I already got?

Enter it in the deposit field. The total, the amount paid and the balance due then print on the file.

Can I charge a late fee?

Print the rate from your contract on the invoice. Check the rule for your state.

Do I keep a copy of each invoice?

Keep it with your records. The IRS says to keep records for 3 years, unless one of its named longer periods applies.
