Yes, exactly. When you purchase the Lifetime Pass for $39, your Apple ID or web account receives permanent Pro status with zero monthly or annual renewal fees. You own your software.
Does it work completely offline on job sites? +
Yes. InvoiceSnap is built local-first on SQLite. Everything — creating estimates, logging material costs, calculating taxes, and generating PDFs — functions seamlessly in basements or rural areas without cellular or WiFi reception.
How does InvoiceSnap compare to Joist and Jobber? +
Joist and Jobber charge ongoing monthly subscriptions ($15 to $99/mo) and rely heavily on continuous cloud connections. InvoiceSnap gives you photo-to-estimate AI, deposit splits, receipt markups, and offline reliability for a single $39 payment.
Can I customize my invoices with my own business logo and colors? +
Absolutely. You can upload your existing high-res logo or use our built-in AI logo designer. You can also specify custom payment notes, bank wire details, Zelle/Venmo handles, and terms.
Can I export and share invoices via WhatsApp or text message? +
Yes. With one tap, InvoiceSnap renders a crisp vector PDF document and opens the standard iOS share sheet so you can send it via iMessage, WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
