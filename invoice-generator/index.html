What you type stays in your browser. The form runs in your browser and makes the files on your device. It does not send form values to us. We count page visits and downloads with Google Analytics. If you close the page, the form data is gone. The files are PDF, Word and Excel, not an e-invoice format.

Who this form is for

Small businesses and self-employed people who need one invoice now and do not want a new account. Trades that bill parts and labor can tax each part in their own way. People who bill only time can use the freelance invoice, and trade workers can start from a page with real line items, such as contractor or plumbing.

What you get

  • A PDF on US Letter paper.
  • An editable Word file.
  • An Excel file with line totals and sales tax as formulas.
  • A blank PDF to print and fill in by hand.

What to check

The form does the math and prints your text. It does not check tax rates or decide what a state asks for. Read what to include on an invoice if this is your first one.

When a form is not enough

The InvoiceSnap app saves customers and past invoices. It records payments that you get outside the app. It drafts estimate lines from a job description with optional online AI, and you review each draft. The free plan has 3 invoices and 3 estimates in total, not per month. The app has 19 invoice designs. Plans and prices are in the app. The form on this page has fields that the app does not have, such as a service date.
